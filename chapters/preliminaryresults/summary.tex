This chapter presented the preliminary results for this work.
These results consist of analysis of individual \glspl{DNP}' yields, concentrations, and count rates; various sensitivities, and verification of \gls{DNP} group parameters with results in the literature.
The 0D scaled model results have good agreement with the literature for the longer-lived groups, but has poor agreement for the shorter-lived groups.
The sensitivities include analysis of chemical removal rates, time nodes, and nuclear data uncertainties.
These sensitivities are useful for determining parameters for the 0D scaled model, understanding how the chemical removal affects the \gls{DNP} group parameters, and analyzing how each group responds to changes in nuclear data based on its uncertainty.
