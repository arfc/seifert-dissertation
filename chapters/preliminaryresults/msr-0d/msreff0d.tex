The in-core residence time is defined as a minimum, as based on the net residence times, the cycle may not complete.
For example, Figure \ref{fig:br-irrad} shows the irradiation of a sample for various combinations of in-core and ex-core residence times.
The net irradiation time is 420 seconds, for which 10, 20, and 30 are all multiples.
However, 40 is not, which leads to a situation where the concentrations from the 20 second in-core and ex-core irradiation are from the incorrect cycle.
To account for this, the irradiation time is instead treated as a minimum irradiation time, and the actual irradiation time will be increased to allot for the full cycle to complete.
