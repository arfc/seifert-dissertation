This is the lowest fidelity of the three models, and it primarily serves as a way to verify the microscopic approach is working as expected by comparing with existing literature.
This approach can only model saturation concentrations, as there is no decay chain tracking nuclides decaying into each other.
It is called the 0D scaled model because it uses the same concept from the scaled flux method, discussed in Section \ref{sec:bateman}.
Instead, the cumulative fission yield is used to calculate the equilibrium \gls{DNP} concentrations, with a scaling term based on the amount of fuel salt in the core.
An example solution with no reprocessing is shown in Equation \eqref{eq:cfy-conc} as \cite{huynh2014calculation}:
\begin{equation}
    N_i = \frac{f_{in} CFY_i}{\lambda_i},
    \label{eq:cfy-conc}
\end{equation}
where,
\begin{align}
    f_{in} &= \text{fraction of fuel-salt in the core} \nonumber \\
    CFY_i &= \text{cumulative fission yield of nuclide $i$}. \nonumber
\end{align}
The derivation for this equation is given in Appendix \ref{sec:appendixB}.

Another consideration is that no parasitic absorption is accounted for in this model, nor is time dependent decay of nuclides into \glspl{DNP}.
There is a possible approach to include the effects of parasitic absorption, but this method is not included in this work \cite{shahbazi_steady-state_2022, benedictnuclear}.

Figure \ref{fig:data-model} shows the different data loaded into \gls{MoSDeN}, including external data and user defined input data.
The terms in green represent input data that should be considered constant for the \gls{DNP} group parameters.
The yellow terms indicate inputs that can be freely adjusted to formulate physically distinct \gls{DNP} group parameters.

After all this data is loaded into \gls{MoSDeN}, it generates the concentration of each \gls{DNP}.
This concentration is then combined with emission probabilities and decay constants to form a delayed neutron count rate.
A non-linear least squares solve then fits the improved \gls{DNP} group parmeters to the delayed neutron count rate, generating the group yields and half-lives, given as blue squares in Figure \ref{fig:data-model}.
