To create tabulated \gls{DNP} group parameter data in a reactor agnostic manner, the manner of tabulation needs to be reactor agnostic as well.
At the same time, a large number of tabulation parameters to fully detail the flow regime is not computationally feasible, and is not necessary to capture the effects of flow.
Instead, the tabulation uses only three parameters: a mixing rate ($\lambda_{mix}$), an in-core average residence time ($\tau_{in}$), and an ex-core residence time ($\tau_{ex}$).

This approach captures the advection of the fuel salt around the primary loop as well as turbulent and diffusive mixing.
The terms are based on the averaged behavior from a control volume of fuel salt, so thermal-hydraulic effects such as vortices would be treated as changes in residence times and mixing rate.
This method thus allows an approximate fitting for any circulating fuel reactor geometry, where all that is required is generating the relevant parameters for accessing the tabulated data.
