This chapter details the tools and methods used to both generate the improved \gls{DNP} group parameters and to validate them.
Three models are covered in this chapter: the 0D scaled model, which is already complete; the 0D flow model, which is proposed; and the spatially resolved model, which is discussed for potential avenues of future research.
Although each model varies in its fidelity and cost, the general methodology is very similar: generate concentrations of \glspl{DNP} from irradiation of a sample, convert those concentrations into a delayed neutron count rate, and then use non-linear least squares to fit \gls{DNP} group parameters to the count rate curve.
Validation of the \gls{DNP} group parameters requires solving kinetics problems that make use of the parameters.
There are also transient problems of interest that affect safety, security, and safeguards by design.
