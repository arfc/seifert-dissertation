Overview of rest of document

1939 Roberts et al. discovery of delayed neutrons \cite{roberts1939further}.
1946 initial spectral measurements \cite{burgy1946energies}.
In 1947, Hughes et al. initial use of six groups (kinda) at Argonne heavy water pile \cite{hughes1948delayed}.
In 1957, Keepin used a sample of U235 in Godiva to generate \gls{DNP} group parameters (improved group fit).
Thus far, only macroscopic approaches have been possible due to insufficient data.
For MSRs which have chemical removal of certain elements, tracking individual nuclides is important.
In 1989, Brady's thesis used updated data on 271 nuclides identified as \glspl{DNP} (first attempt at microscopic/summation/aggregate approach).
This data was used for ENDF-B/VI was released, and since then there have not been any changes to the \gls{DNP} group parameters \cite{wilson2002delayed, Brown20181}.
In 1999, Parish says "Based on the six-group data reported in the literature, it appears that the ENDF/B-VI six-group constants are in need of revision." \cite{parish1999status}.
In 2014, \gls{IAEA} \gls{CRP} on Development of a Reference Database for
$\beta$-delayed neutron emission data \cite{mills2014calculation}.
2020 Leconte uses more than 400 DNPs with data \cite{leconte2020new}.
