This chapter presented the background and motivation for this work. 
\Glspl{DNP} were introduced, and the methods used to form \gls{DNP} groups were described. 
The equations used to calculate the \gls{DNP} group half-lives ($\tau_k$), yields ($\bar{\nu}_{d,k}$), and spectra ($\chi_{d,k}$) were also provided.
Kinetic parameters and various approaches for solving kinetics problems were discussed, as these parameters and methods provide essential background information for the safety, security, and safeguards transient simulations introduced in this work.
Overall, this chapter provided background information on how improved \gls{DNP} group parameters can be created for \glspl{MSR}, as well as motivation for why this improvement is important.
