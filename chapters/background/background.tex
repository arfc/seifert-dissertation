This chapter provides the background and motivation for generating more accurate \gls{DNP} group parameters for \glspl{MSR} and implementation of those parameters for safety, security, and safeguards purposes.
An overview, including information on \glspl{DNP} and \gls{MSR} fuel depletion, is followed by relevant context motivating the problem, including safety, security, and safeguards.
The \gls{DNP} group parameters in \glspl{MSR} differ from traditional group parameters in stationary systems because the fuel circulates and undergoes online chemical reprocessing, which both alter the local density and distributions of \glspl{DNP}.
These effects can be captured in spatially resolved \gls{MSR} fuel depletion simulations.
Existing methodologies for \gls{DNP} group parameter generation do not account for the recirculation and chemical removal present in \gls{MSR} fuel depletion \cite{keepin1957delayed, tuttle1975delayed, manero1972status, benedetti1982delayed, waldo1981delayed, brady1988evaluation, hughes1948delayed, loaiza1998measurements, loaiza2000dominant, pfeiffer2002status, d2002review, pfeiffer2002status, rudstam2002delayed, wilson2002delayed, brady1989delayed, leconte2020new}.
This work presents improved \gls{DNP} group parameters that account for recirculation and chemical removal.
These improved \gls{DNP} group parameters are particularly important for safety, security, and safeguards related concerns.
