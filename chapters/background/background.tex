This work aims to generate more accurate \gls{DNP} group parameters for \glspl{MSR} and implement those parameters for safety, security, and safeguards transient scenarios.
These \gls{DNP} group parameters for \glspl{MSR} differ due to physical phenomena in \glspl{MSR}, specifically the chemical removal of specific elements and circulation of the fuel salt.
These effects can be captured in spatially resolved \gls{MSR} fuel depletion simulations.

Overall, safety, security, and safeguards motivate this work by providing justification for why higher accuracy in the \gls{DNP} group parameters is necessary.
The existing methodology for \gls{DNP} group parameter generation does not account for the physical phenomena present in \gls{MSR} fuel depletion, which is why this section covers background for \glspl{DNP} and \gls{MSR} fuel depletion.
By accounting for these physical phenomena, the newly generated \gls{DNP} group parameters offer higher accuracy for transients, which is particularly important for safety, security, and safeguards related concerns.
