This work investigates the formulation of new \gls{DNP} group parameters, which impact the reactor behavior during a transient.
There may be security issues that arise during a sabotage-initiated transient due to the improved kinetics and \gls{DNP} group parameters.

The goal of security, or \gls{PP}, is to prevent actors from acquiring information, stealing nuclear material, or committing sabotage \cite{protection2011evaluation}.
Security is enforced through physical protection systems, which detect, delay, and neutralize the adversarial actor before they can complete their objective \cite{army2001physical, garcia2007design}.
There are several different categories which are important to understanding security: the actor type, their capabilities, their objectives, and their strategies.
The main consideration for security in this work is protection of the public by preventing: theft of hazardous radioactive material and sabotage with the intent to damage facilities, disrupt operations, or release radioactive material.
The majority of the information presented in this section comes from the Proliferation Resistance and Physical Protection Evaluation Methodology Working Group of the Generation IV International Forum \cite{protection2011evaluation}.

The actor type involved in \gls{PP} can be an individual, a group, a large organization, or a non-host state combined with any of the former.
These actors may be composed of some combination of insiders and outsiders.
Insiders are employees or contract staff who may be sympathetic with or suborned by the hostile actor.
Outsiders can include terrorist groups, agents of proliferant states, or lone individuals.
Based on the specifics of the actor type, their capabilities will vary significantly.

Actor capabilities can be classified into five categories: knowledge, skills, weapons and tools, number of actors, and dedication.
The knowledge of the actor will vary depending on if they have insider knowledge or not, and how much information that insider knows.
The skills of the actor will affect their strategy they use to achieve their goals, though a large organization or non-host state can be assumed to have access to a wide variety of skills.
The weapons and tools of the actor will affect their strategy, but access to an insider would grant more tools accessed with less effort.
The number of actors will vary with the specific strategy of that actor as well as their tactics, as a more discrete approach would require fewer actors than a forceful approach.
The dedication of the actor is important to consider, as an insufficiently motivated actor may be deterred with sufficient security measures.

The objectives of the actor may include: disruption of operations, radiological release, nuclear explosives, \glspl{RDD}, and/or information theft.
The objectives of the actor will influence their other decisions, particularly their strategies.
If an actor's objective requires force, then the capabilities and strategies implemented by that actor will vary from one whose objective can be accomplished covertly.
Assuming the actor is rational, they will select a strategy which optimizes the probability of successfully completing their objective.

The actor's strategies include their modes of attack and tactics. The modes of attack vary significantly, including cyber attacks, waterborne attacks, direct or diversionary attack using light anti-tank weapons, and more.
The tactics actors can use include stealth, deceit, and overt force.
The stealth and deceit tactics are more covert, including avoiding or deactivating security systems for the stealth tactic or using false identification or authorization for the deceit tactic.
The overt force tactic ties more directly into many of the modes of attack, which may itself also serve as a diversion for a different tactic.


For this work, only a single type of actor and objective combination is considered -- an actor with access to an insider and the objective to disrupt operations through sabotage.
The specific actor capabilities and strategies are not considered, only the actor type and objective.
