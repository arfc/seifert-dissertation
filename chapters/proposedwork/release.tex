The final proposed extension to this work is the release of an open-source Python package.
This package will provide users with the capability to generate \gls{DNP} group parameters for any \gls{MSR}, with any fissile nuclide, energy spectra, and desired number of \gls{DNP} groups.
The plan is to offer a customizable and modular tool which is easily accessible, enabling users to study more effects of these updated \gls{DNP} group parameters.

There are many additional effects to study which would be of interest, including the following: more refined data generation, investigation of other fissile nuclides and energy spectra, further optimizations to the methodology, and inclusion of more \gls{MSR} physical phenomena such as thermochemistry.
Other future work of interest would be experimental results replicating the methodology proposed in this work, specifically, irradiation of a fissile sample in a circulating loop and counting of the delayed neutrons from that sample.
