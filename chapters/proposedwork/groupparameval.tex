The evaluation of the \gls{DNP} group parameters is vital, as the parameters should be both accurate and usable.
To evaluate the accuracy of the parameters, they are validated using macroscopic experimental data as well as \gls{MSRE} transients.
To evaluate the usability of the parameters, modified versions of the \gls{MSRE} and \gls{MSFR} are evaluated using the spatially resolved flow model.
The results of these evaluations will demonstrate the validity of the \gls{DNP} group parameters as well as their use cases.
