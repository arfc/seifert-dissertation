It is vital to evaluate the \gls{DNP} group parameters to ensure they are accurate and usable.
%Evaluating the \gls{DNP} group parameters is vital, as they must be both accurate and usable.
To assess accuracy, the parameters are validated using macroscopic experimental data and \gls{MSRE} transients.
To evaluate usability, modified versions of the \gls{MSRE} and \gls{MSFR} are analyzed using the spatially resolved flow model.
These evaluations demonstrate the validity and practical applications of the \gls{DNP} group parameters.
