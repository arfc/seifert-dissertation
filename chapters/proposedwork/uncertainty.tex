There are several sources of uncertainty which can be considered, including those from half-lives, emission probabilities, cross sections, and fission yields.
These uncertainties propagate and results in uncertainty in the \gls{DNP} group and kinetics parameters \cite{radaideh2019new}.
The uncertainties in the \gls{DNP} group parameters are tracked through the non-linear least squares fitting by taking the square root of the generated covariance matrix.
This uncertainty uses a linear approximation near the optimum \cite{vugrin2007confidence}.

There are also uncertainties in the \gls{MSR} physical phenomena.
These include uncertainties in the flow rate and reprocessing rates, where the reprocessing rate is correlated with the flow rate.
The magnitude of this uncertainty is discussed in Section \ref{sec:msr-results}, where the sensitivity of the \gls{DNP} group parameters to the changes in the \gls{MSR} phenomena demonstrate the potential impact of uncertainties.
