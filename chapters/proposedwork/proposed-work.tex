Completion of the proposed work will result in a complete analysis, from start to finish, of improved \gls{DNP} group parameters for thermal fission of $^{235}$U and the effects of these improved parameters on transient simulations.
\Gls{MoSDeN}, which will be completed with this work, will serve as a tool to investigate the \gls{DNP} group parameters with \gls{MSR} physical phenomena included.
The proposed work is not exhaustive, and will not investigate every fissile nuclide, nor every irradiation energy.
Work which is deemed interesting yet not critical is included in Section \ref{sec:future-work}.

The critical work includes developing the 0D flow model, validating and verifying the results of the 0D flow model, generating improved group spectra along with the \gls{DNP} group parameters, and finally using the \gls{DNP} group parameters in transient simulations.
The non-critical work includes development of the spatially resolved model, uncertainty tracking of \gls{MSR} physical phenomena in the \gls{DNP} group parameters, and investigation into the effects of varying the incident neutron energy spectrum on the \gls{DNP} group parameters.
