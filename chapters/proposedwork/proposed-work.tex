Completion of the proposed work will result in a complete analysis, from start to finish, of improved \gls{DNP} group parameters for thermal fission of $^{235}$U and the effects of these improved parameters on transient simulations.
\Gls{MoSDeN}, which will be completed with this work, will serve as a tool to investigate the \gls{DNP} group parameters with \gls{MSR} physical phenomena included.
The proposed work is not exhaustive, and will not investigate every fissile nuclide, nor every irradiation energy.
Work which is deemed interesting yet not critical is included in Section \ref{sec:future-work}.

The critical work includes developing the 0D flow model, validating and verifying the results of the 0D flow model, generating improved group spectra along with the \gls{DNP} group parameters, and finally using the \gls{DNP} group parameters in transient simulations.
The non-critical work includes development of the spatially resolved model, uncertainty tracking of \gls{MSR} physical phenomena in the \gls{DNP} group parameters, and investigation into the effects of varying the incident neutron energy spectrum on the \gls{DNP} group parameters.

Figure \ref{fig:gantt-prelim} shows the critical work path, divided into development, simulation, writing, and review categories.
The categories which require more focus are development and writing, whereas simulation and review is generally a more passive process.
Most tasks generally take one month, but three tasks are assumed to take two months: development of the \gls{MSRE} Moltres model, running the various \gls{MSRE} transients, and writing the results and conclusions chapters.
Development of the Moltres model is expected to take longer because there may be unexpected issues with the meshing of the upper and lower plena, data collection, or model development itself.
The \gls{MSRE} transient simulations are given two months of simulation time because there are 12 transient simulations for safety, security, and safeguards simulations.
These transient simulations also require five transport solves: three for standard \gls{MSRE} operation prior to a transient with higher and lower temperatures, one with one \gls{SQ} removal of $^{235}$U over one year, and one with one \gls{SQ} removal of Pu over one year.
The results chapter is given two months because there will be many new results to discuss, whereas the other chapters will not have significant changes.
