The \gls{DNP} group spectra will be generated using using the half-life grouping and fractional grouping methods.
The delayed neutron energy spectrum, $\chi_d$ is important for accurate prediction of reactor kinetics \cite{das1993importance}.
This can be seen in the equation for the effective delayed neutron fraction in Equation \eqref{eq:betaeff-exact}, recreated here as:
\begin{equation}
    \beta_{eff} = \frac{\int \Phi^\dagger \chi_d \beta \bar{\nu} \Sigma_f \Phi \; dE d\vec{\Omega} d\vec{r}}{\int \Phi^\dagger \chi \bar{\nu} \Sigma_f \Phi \; dE d\vec{\Omega} d\vec{r}}.
    \nonumber
\end{equation}
Because the \gls{DNP} group yields and half-lives are affected by the \gls{MSR} physical phenomena, it follows that the \gls{DNP} group spectra are also affected.
The methodology to evaluate the delayed neutron spectra is discussed in Section \ref{sec:groupspectra}.
The \gls{DNP} group spectra will be compared with the spectra generated from a stationary problem with no chemical removal to determine the effect of \gls{MSR} phenomena on the spectra.
