  \frametitle{Fuel salt recirculation affects concentration of \glspl{DNP}}
        \begin{columns}
                \column[t]{5cm}
                Although normalized per fission, changes in the fission rate due to recirculation changes transmutation rates which could affect \gls{DNP} concentrations.
                
                \begin{block}{In-core and ex-core salt circulation can be modeled using:}
                    \begin{itemize}
                        \item Explicit modeling
                        \item Scaled flux method \cite{betzler_liquid-fueled_2021}
                    \end{itemize}
                \end{block}

                \vspace{2mm}

                \textbf{For a circulating fissile sample, the scaled flux method may not be sufficient, as it is not intended for short time scales \cite{betzler_liquid-fueled_2021}.}
