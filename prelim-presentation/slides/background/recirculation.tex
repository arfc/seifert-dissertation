  \frametitle{Fuel salt recirculation affects concentration of \glspl{DNP}}
        \begin{columns}
                \column[t]{5cm}
                Although normalized per fission, changes in the fission rate due to recirculation changes transmutation rates which could affect \gls{DNP} concentrations.
                
                \begin{block}{In-core and ex-core salt recirculation can be modeled using:}
                    \begin{itemize}
                        \item \textbf{Scaled flux method} \cite{betzler_liquid-fueled_2021}
			\item \textbf{Piecewise 0D method}
			\item Spatially resolved method \cite{tano2023coupled,seifert_luke_computationally_2025}
                    \end{itemize}
                \end{block}

                \vspace{2mm}

                \textbf{For a recirculating fissile sample, the piecewise 0D model offers a good balance of accuracy and speed.}
